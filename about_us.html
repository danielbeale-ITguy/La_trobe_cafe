Our Values

At La Trobe Cafe, we pride ourselves on a welcoming, inclusive place for students, who want to recharge, and feel at home. Our values center around good coffee and good times, committed to serving fresh coffee, cakes, bread and other breakfast food in a cozy environment. We believe in supporting local, creating a hub that not only satisfies your caffeine needs but help with creativity.

Atmosphere

The La Trobe Cafe is a cozy and inviting space, perfect for students looking to get-away and focus on their studies. With its warm brick walls, and soft lighting, the cafe creates a cozy and welcoming atmosphere. Large windows, and shelves filled with books and plants add to calm student nerves. The smell of coffee and the delicious pastries can help any student with their classes.

Barista

Lead Barista: Daniel Beale, one half of the duo who run La Trobe Café. A student at La Trobe University and co-owner of the café, with years of experience drinking coffee — now finally making it!

Baker

Head Baker: Lily La-Trobe, the second half of the La Trobe Café duo. With years of experience and a love for desserts, she helps keep this busy café running with fresh baked goods every day.
