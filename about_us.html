Our Values

Welcome to La Trobe Café – a vibrant, student-run hub nestled in the heart of Melbourne, just steps away from the bustling La Trobe University campus. Born from a passion for great coffee, community connection, and student empowerment, our café is more than just a place to grab a cuppa – it’s a space where ideas brew as richly as our espresso. Run entirely by students, for students, La Trobe Café offers a unique blend of quality food, sustainably sourced coffee, and a welcoming atmosphere that celebrates creativity, collaboration, and campus life. Whether you're cramming for exams, meeting friends, or simply taking a well-deserved break, our friendly team is here to make your visit enjoyable and energizing. We pride ourselves on fostering a supportive environment where students gain real-world experience in hospitality, business, and leadership – all while serving up delicious, affordable meals and drinks. From hearty breakfast bowls to vegan treats and barista-made lattes, our menu is crafted with care and catered to busy student lifestyles. Located conveniently near the La Trobe University campus in Melbourne’s north, La Trobe Café is your go-to spot for good vibes, great food, and a true taste of student spirit. Come for the coffee.
